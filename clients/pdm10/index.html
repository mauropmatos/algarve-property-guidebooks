Property

  • Set the temperature using the remote or wall panel. Press the power button, use the up/down arrows to adjust temperature. For cooling set between 20 and 23 degrees. Turn off when leaving.
  • Press the power button to activate. Adjust temperature using the plus and minus buttons. Recommended setting is 21 degrees. Turn off when leaving.
  • Bosch WGG256Z1ES. Place one pod in the drum before adding clothes. Select your programme and press start. Standard cycle is 40 degrees.
  • Bosch SMS4HTI16E. Place one tablet in the dispenser. Load dishes and select a programme. Press start.
  • Turn the left dial to select the function. Turn the right dial to set the temperature. Allow 10 minutes to preheat. Turn both dials back to zero when finished.
  • Capsules and machine are on the kitchen counter. Insert a capsule, place your cup, and press the button. French press and ground coffee are also available in the cupboard above the kettle.
  • Hot water is instant throughout the apartment. No need to switch anything on.
  • If you lose power, open the fuse box panel and check if any switches have flipped down. Push them back up to restore power. Call the host if the problem persists.
  • Leave the building through the main entrance. Turn right toward the beach. The bins and recycling containers are on the right side, approximately 30 metres ahead.
  • You may park in any unmarked space within the complex. Do not park in spaces with apartment numbers marked on the ground.
